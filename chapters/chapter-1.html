THE FORGOTTEN FIELD · CHAPTER 01

A Serpent in the Darkness

A chapter companion by ChaChiLu

⚠ Chapter spoilers ahead. This page contains a fan-written recap and commentary, not the complete novel chapter.

In the stillness of the imperial gardens, Crown Prince Gareth awaits Varkas Shierkhan and the results of an inquiry into the substance slipped into Princess Ayla's wine. The findings, however, are not quite what he expects.

Varkas reports that the medicine was not obtained from the Empress's Palace. Instead, the investigation points towards the Second Princess, Thalia. Gareth's fury is immediate, yet Varkas calmly explains why the evidence may be insufficient to secure any serious punishment.

As Gareth's anger rises, Varkas remains unnervingly composed. He urges the prince not to devote so much attention to the matter and makes it clear that he wishes no further involvement.

Then Gareth asks a question that changes the atmosphere entirely: could Varkas be protecting Thalia? The suggestion provokes a rare flash of unmistakable emotion. Varkas declares that he never wishes to be involved with her again.

Gareth interprets this reaction as proof that Varkas's hostility towards Thalia will serve their political alliance. But the scene leaves readers with a far more interesting question: what lies beneath Varkas's carefully guarded expression?
